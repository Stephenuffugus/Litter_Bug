How to play
The goal
Work the alley for Shinies, and every thirty Shinies buys you one bug that has never existed before and never will again. Fill the Bugdex.
How it plays
SCAVENGE gives you a sixty second job on one of three blocks. Sort the recycling, hunt a grub out of a junk pile, or untangle the wires. Each pays Shinies, and there is a daily limit so the alley is worth coming back to.
The controls
  • Sorting: tap the bin that matches the falling junk
  • Grub hunt: tap the grub hiding in the pile
  • Wires: drag a pin until no two wires cross
  • Every bug you mint is graded and kept in the Bugdex
  • The arrow at the bottom left returns to the arcade
Which block?
Sixty seconds each.
