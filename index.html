How to play
The goal
Work the alley for Shinies, and every thirty Shinies buys you one bug that has never existed before and never will again. Fill the Bugdex.
How it plays
SCAVENGE gives you a sixty second job on one of four blocks. Sort the recycling, hunt a grub out of a junk pile, untangle the wires, or pry the lids. Four shifts a day, and a clean shift is about one bug.
The dumpster
Five challengers turn up every day, the same five for everybody. Pick a move, read what the other bug is winding up, and win. Every win feeds your champion and it grows. Beat all five and you are King of the Dumpster.
The controls
  • Sorting: tap the bin that matches the ringed piece
  • Grub hunt: tap the grub hiding in the pile
  • Wires: drag a pin until no two wires cross
  • Lids: tap PRY while the marker sits on the seam
  • Every bug you mint is graded and kept in the Bugdex
  • The arrow at the bottom left returns to the arcade
Which block?
Sixty seconds each.
